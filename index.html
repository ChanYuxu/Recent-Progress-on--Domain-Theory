  1. 16 Oct 2026

    Scott topology, core-compactness and sobriety

    A Non-Sober Core-Compact Meet-Continuous dcpo

    A meet-continuous dcpo has a compact, second-countable and core-compact Scott space that is neither sober nor locally compact. The expanded supplement compares six pointed orders with a directly presented example without a least element.

    Formalization boundary The Lean development covers the mathematical content of arXiv v1, while its hash-bound audit targets a pre-upload draft with editorial differences. The supplement is not formalized; human review is pending.

  2. 13 Oct 2026

    Chain approximation and Scott-closed tests

    Chain Continuity and Scott-Closed Interpolation

    On a chain-continuous dcpo, the square of chain-below is ordinary way-below. A Jónsson algebra makes the square strict; a separate lower-set lattice defeats Scott-closed interpolation.

    Formalization boundary The independently reviewed Lean project covers an earlier manuscript snapshot with 19 numbered results. arXiv v1 adds a countably based proposition and a Scott-basis conclusion that have not been source-audited in Lean. Human review is pending.
