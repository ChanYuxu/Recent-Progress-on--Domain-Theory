A Non-Sober Core-Compact Meet-Continuous dcpo

Yuxu Chen · arXiv:2610.22033v1 · math.GN

All content in this section is AI-generated. AI may make mistakes; please verify important information carefully.

Core-compactness is a topological approximation property weaker than local compactness. This paper constructs a meet-continuous dcpo whose Scott space is core-compact but neither sober nor locally compact, separating these notions inside a particularly structured class of dcpos.

Main theorem. There are a locally compact sober complete lattice L and a proper sub-dcpo D such that the Scott-closed-set lattices of L and D are isomorphic, while L and D are not order-isomorphic. The Scott space of D is compact, second countable and core-compact, but it is not sober or locally compact.

From a transfer principle to the counterexample

The argument first isolates two approximation conditions under which restriction along a sub-dcpo induces inverse frame isomorphisms on Scott opens. These conditions also transfer core-compactness and second countability and yield intrinsic meet-continuity. The concrete dcpo is then obtained inside a compact topological lattice by a transfinite insertion construction designed to satisfy both conditions while excluding a greatest point.

The resulting example gives a negative answer to the question whether every core-compact meet-continuous dcpo is sober. Together with the sobriety theorem for second-countable locally compact dcpos used in the paper, non-sobriety also forces failure of local compactness. The same construction shows that a locally compact sober dcpo need not be determined, among all dcpos, by its Scott-closed-set lattice.

Expanded supplementary material

The expanded supplement is published anonymously. It develops four finite-configuration constructions and two pointed positive-height variants. Section S6 gives a direct construction without a least element, with coordinate proofs of core-compactness and non-sobriety. The extended comparison lists seven orders; with compatible choices, the unpointed example is the positive-height part of an earlier construction, so it is not claimed as a seventh independent construction scheme. The supplement also studies locally compact points, compact elements and countable cofinality. These results are separate from the Lean coverage described below.
